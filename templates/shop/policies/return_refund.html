Return Policy

At Shuddho, we want our customers to receive products in perfect condition. Please read our return policy carefully before accepting your order.

🔍 Product Checking at Delivery

Customers are requested to check the product carefully while the delivery person is present. Please verify the product condition before accepting the delivery.

📦 Return Conditions

If the delivered product is damaged, defective, or significantly different from the displayed product image, you may request a return according to our return conditions. Applicable courier charges may apply.

⏰ Return Time Limit

Any return request or complaint must be made immediately during delivery inspection. Once the delivery person leaves, returns or complaints may not be accepted.

⚠️ Important Notes

Products must be returned in their original condition. Items that are used, damaged by customers, or missing original packaging may not qualify for return.
